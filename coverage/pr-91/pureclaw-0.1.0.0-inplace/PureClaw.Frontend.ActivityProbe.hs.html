    1 -- | Activity probe loop — now a thin shim over the registry-based reconcile
    2 -- loop ("PureClaw.Harness.Reconcile", WU5 of the Harness Registry feature).
    3 --
    4 -- /History./ This module originally walked the process-wide
    5 -- @IORef (Map Text HarnessHandle)@ every two seconds and published one
    6 -- 'PureClaw.Frontend.StreamBroker.ActivityChanged' per state transition. That
    7 -- legacy probe had a structural gap: it used a one-way map diff, so a harness
    8 -- that /disappeared/ between ticks emitted no event (the bug at the old
    9 -- @ActivityProbe.hs:117@).
   10 --
   11 -- /Now./ The 'PureClaw.Harness.Registry' is the source of truth for harness
   12 -- identity + health, and 'PureClaw.Harness.Reconcile' owns the loop: it sweeps
   13 -- tmux, corroborates ownership by PID (§8 C4), classifies liveness, and emits a
   14 -- /symmetric/-diff stream of 'ActivityChanged' events that INCLUDES
   15 -- disappearances. This module re-exports that loop under the historical name so
   16 -- the CLI wiring in "PureClaw.CLI.Commands" reads unchanged at the call site.
   17 --
   18 -- The loop's behavioural contract (first-tick baseline, cancellation re-raise,
   19 -- transient-failure resilience) and its deterministic test seam now live in
   20 -- "PureClaw.Harness.Reconcile" ('Reconcile.runReconcileLoopWith') and are
   21 -- covered by @test\/Harness\/ReconcileSpec.hs@.
